# Description of the Incident and Damage

## 1. Initial Situation

Before the work began, there was already a loose connection in the neutral conductor area inside the existing old meter cabinet.

The old cabinet was technically outdated, poorly organized, and had tangled, unstructured wiring. The neutral conductor terminal already showed looseness and instability during the visual inspection.

For the planned transfer of the meter into the new meter cabinet, a power shutdown of approximately 30 minutes was scheduled.

---

## 2. Sequence of the Incident

The power supply was disconnected and the meter was removed from the old cabinet.

While removing the meter, the already damaged neutral conductor was mechanically moved again due to the unstable old installation. This caused a loose connection / interruption in the neutral conductor within the old wiring area.

After completing the installation in the new meter cabinet, the system was re-energized.

Because of the interrupted neutral conductor, voltage irregularities / overvoltage spikes occurred in individual electrical circuits when the power was switched back on.

As a result, the circuit breakers tripped. At the same time, damage occurred to connected household appliances.

---

# 3. Affected Devices

The following household appliances were damaged due to the neutral conductor fault / voltage surge:
